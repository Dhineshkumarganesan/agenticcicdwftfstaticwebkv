Intent-Driven Agentic CI/CD: How Natural Language Drives Your Entire Pipeline from Scaffolding to Deployment Traditional CI/CD pipelines are often rigid and require developers to explicitly define every build, test, and deployment step, making it hard to scale across multiple projects or rapidly changing requirements. Intent-driven CI/CD solves this by understanding what you want to deploy and automatically scaffolding the necessary pipeline steps, reducing manual configuration, minimizing errors, and speeding up delivery. This approach is especially valuable in complex, multi-repo, or agentic AI environments where deployments must be dynamic, repeatable, and aligned with business intent.
